# 🚀 Sourcing Evaluator Agent - AI-Powered Candidate Evaluation

## What is It?

**Sourcing Evaluator** is an AI-powered tool that evaluates candidates 10x faster than manual screening, aligned with **Mambu's Career Framework**.

Instead of spending 12 minutes per candidate on manual evaluation, get structured, data-driven assessments in seconds.

---

## 🎯 Core Features

### **1. Evaluator Tab** - AI Candidate Assessment
Paste a job description, add candidates, get instant evaluations.

**Output includes:**
- ✅ Match Score (0-100%)
- ✅ Mambu Level (0-8) - Aligned with your internal leveling
- ✅ Career Path Assessment (IC vs Team Leader)
- ✅ Technical Fit Summary
- ✅ Strengths & Red Flags
- ✅ Growth Potential Timeline
- ✅ Interview Questions
- ✅ Hiring Recommendation

**Example Output:**
```
Sofia Chen
- Match: 95%
- Level: 5 (Experienced IC)
- Growth: "Promotion to Level 4 or TL within 12-18 months"
- Recommendation: STRONG YES
```

### **2. Candidate Finder Tab** - LinkedIn Search Generation
Generate optimized LinkedIn search queries automatically.

- Paste JD → AI creates 7 targeted search queries
- Ideal candidate profile description
- Search tips & strategies

### **3. Analytics Tab** - Team Insights & ROI
See your team's impact in real-time.

- Total evaluations & active users
- Average match scores
- Conversion rate predictions by Mambu Level
- Time saved calculation
- Recommendations breakdown
